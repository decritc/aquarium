# Economy and Progression

## Design goals

- Support a satisfying idle progression curve.
- Keep sandbox mode free from progression constraints.
- Let players earn meaningful upgrades without mandatory spending.
- Monetize through convenience, expression, and acceleration rather than hard power walls.

## Currencies

### Soft currency: Credits

Used for:

- Buying common fish
- Purchasing standard decorations and plants
- Unlocking larger tanks
- Paying breeding, maintenance, and logistics costs

Sources:

- Selling fish
- Daily tank income from public popularity and completed tank goals
- Missions, contracts, and achievements
- Passive breeder business revenue

### Premium currency: Pearls

Used for:

- Time acceleration
- Quality-of-life upgrades
- Premium cosmetics
- Limited convenience actions

Sources:

- Real-money purchases
- Small gameplay rewards
- Events or tutorials

### Optional third currency: Tokens

Reserved for seasonal events or special shops if needed later.

## Breeder career progression

### Starting state

- One small freshwater starter tank
- Very limited starting bankroll
- Access to basic food, filtration, and decorations
- Access to a few hardy starter fish species

### Progression pillars

1. **Tank expansion**
   - More tank slots
   - Larger tanks
   - Specialized environments

2. **Species access**
   - Unlock higher rarity fish
   - Unlock saltwater systems later than freshwater
   - Unlock specialist breeding opportunities

3. **Operational efficiency**
   - Automation for feeding, cleaning, and maintenance
   - Better analytics around breeding and sales
   - Reduced failure risk

4. **Prestige and visibility**
   - Public showcase tanks
   - Popularity rankings
   - Visitor engagement rewards

## Idle loop

The breeder mode should work in short and long sessions.

### Short session loop

- Collect earnings
- Check fish condition
- Feed fish or tune environment
- Review breeding outcomes
- List fish for sale or place them in tanks

### Long session loop

- Build a profitable breeding line
- Specialize in certain species or aesthetics
- Complete themed tank goals
- Expand into premium or difficult habitats

## Fish sales

Fish value can be based on:

- Species rarity
- Visual traits
- Health score
- Age and maturity
- Breeding lineage
- Market demand

Potential channels:

- Direct NPC sales
- Public market listings
- Contract orders
- Collector requests

## Tank completion goals

Career mode should include structured goals beyond breeding.

Examples:

- Build a highly rated planted freshwater tank
- Create a stable reef-compatible saltwater tank
- Maintain ideal conditions for a rare species for multiple sessions
- Host a tank that reaches visitor popularity milestones

## Monetization guardrails

The game should avoid aggressive monetization patterns.

Preferred approach:

- Ads should be optional reward multipliers or ambient placements
- Ad removal should be a clear one-time or subscription benefit
- Premium currency should accelerate time and reduce friction, not lock core content
- Cosmetic purchases should be attractive and meaningful

Avoid:

- Paywalls on basic core progression
- Premium-only essential maintenance systems
- Punishing timers that feel impossible without spending

## Premium purchase categories

- Ad removal
- Convenience automation
- Extra save slots or aquarium showcases
- Premium decor themes
- Nameplate, lighting, and presentation customization
- Time skips within reasonable caps

## Open balancing questions

- How generous should offline progression be?
- Should market prices be fixed, simulated, or event-driven?
- How much randomness should breeding outcomes have?
- What content should be freshwater-only or saltwater-only?
- Should sandbox mode unlock everything immediately or through creator presets?
