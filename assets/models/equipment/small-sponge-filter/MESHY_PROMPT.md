# Meshy Prompt: Small Sponge Filter

## Asset ID

- `small-sponge-filter`

## Purpose

- Gentle beginner filtration
- Visible in betta and breeder/community starter tanks
- Must read clearly at side-view gameplay distance without stealing visual focus

## Style direction

Follow `/workspace/assets/models/MESHY_STYLE_GUIDE.md`.

Target look:

- cartoon realism
- soft rounded forms
- believable aquarium hardware
- slightly stylized proportions
- polished, premium, browser-friendly

## Primary prompt

Create a stylized but believable **small aquarium sponge filter** for a browser-
based virtual aquarium game. The model should feel clean, readable, and premium
at side-view gameplay distance. Use **cartoon realism**: rounded forms, simplified
mechanical detail, smooth shading, and slightly exaggerated clarity rather than
hard industrial realism.

The filter should include:

- a cylindrical or rectangular dark sponge body
- an uplift tube
- a weighted or suction-cup-supported base
- a compact beginner-friendly silhouette

Visual mood:

- practical and aquarium-authentic
- not grimy, rusty, or industrial
- friendly and toy-like enough to match a cozy life-sim UI

Materials:

- dark charcoal sponge
- muted black or dark gray plastic
- subtle semi-matte finish
- no brand logos or text

Shape rules:

- readable from the side
- modest footprint
- suitable for small tanks
- no micro-detail that vanishes in browser play

## Negative prompt

Avoid:

- hyper-detailed industrial machine parts
- exposed wires or unsafe-looking components
- bright saturated colors that distract from fish
- overly complex tubing
- grunge, slime, rust, or damage
- text labels

## Technical guidance

- optimize for browser delivery
- low to moderate polygon density
- minimal material count
- clean silhouette first
- stable origin and scale

## Render/use guidance

- this is a mounted/rear equipment item
- should sit near rear glass/equipment area
- must look good under calm aquarium lighting
- should not dominate the composition

## Deliverables desired from Meshy workflow

- `model.glb`
- preview render
- any texture set used
