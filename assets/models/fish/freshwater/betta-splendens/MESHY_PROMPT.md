# Meshy Prompt: Betta splendens

## Asset ID

- `betta-splendens`
- species definition: `betta-splendens`
- asset folder: `assets/models/fish/freshwater/betta-splendens/`

## Goal

Create a premium **cartoon realism** 3D fish model of a **male Betta splendens**
for a browser-based virtual aquarium game.

This fish is a **hero/showcase fish**. It must look beautiful in:

- side-view gameplay
- landing-page hero captures
- featured observer-tank screenshots

The style should feel:

- bright
- clean
- charming
- premium
- believable without becoming photoreal

It should **not** look:

- muddy
- overly realistic and grim
- low-poly toy-like
- hyper-detailed in a way that hurts browser performance

## Visual direction

Create a healthy, elegant male betta with:

- flowing fins
- graceful silhouette
- rich saturated coloration
- strong side-view readability
- slightly stylized proportions that improve beauty without becoming fantasy

Canonical first colorway:

- jewel-toned teal / turquoise dominant body
- coral-red fin accents as the primary secondary color
- subtle iridescent transitions that stay bright and premium rather than muddy

Recommended coloration:

- jewel-toned teal / turquoise primary body
- coral-red or crimson accents in the fins
- subtle iridescent transitions

Keep the fins broad and expressive but not physically impossible.

## Shape and silhouette requirements

Prioritize side-view readability.

Important silhouette traits:

- broad rounded tail fin
- distinct dorsal and anal fins
- elegant tapering body
- clear head shape visible from the side

The fish should read instantly as a premium betta even at gameplay camera distance.

## Rendering/material guidance

- clean stylized scales, not noisy micro-detail
- subtle surface variation and soft iridescence
- avoid excessive transparency or shader-heavy fin tricks
- materials should support bright aquarium lighting and good edge readability

## Pose and rig expectations

Neutral export pose:

- fish aligned horizontally
- body facing positive forward direction
- centered cleanly for animation

Rig/animation readiness:

- body must support smooth swim undulation
- fins should support subtle secondary motion
- geometry must deform cleanly during turns and hover states

## Required animation outputs

The asset should be produced with or designed for these clips:

1. **Cruise / Swim Idle**
   - calm forward swimming
   - graceful body undulation
   - fins trailing softly
   - loopable

2. **Turn Left**
   - smooth directional bend
   - readable curve through the body
   - loop not required

3. **Turn Right**
   - mirrored right turn
   - loop not required

4. **Threat Display** (strongly preferred)
   - fins spread wider
   - body slows or hovers
   - slight posture / intimidation energy
   - for territorial behavior

5. **Rest / Hover** (preferred)
   - very slow fin motion
   - minimal drift
   - useful for surface rest and calm display moments

## Behavioral feel reference

This fish is:

- calm when alone
- territorial when provoked
- elegant rather than frantic

Its motion should feel like a beautiful display fish, not a schooling darting fish.

## Camera/readability requirements

The game uses:

- fixed side-view aquarium presentation
- free-rotation edit preview

So the model must:

- look excellent from the side
- still hold up in angled orbit views
- keep strong silhouette when passing in front of or behind decor

## Scale guidance

Approximate real-world body length target:

- about 7 cm adult length

Keep proportions grounded in reality with a slight stylized boost for elegance.

## Performance constraints

- optimized for browser delivery
- avoid excessive mesh density
- avoid too many materials
- keep texture usage reasonable
- prefer strong forms over unnecessary tiny details

## Output expectation

Deliver a production-ready fish model for:

- `model.glb`
- preview render / thumbnail
- clips or clip-ready rig for:
  - cruise
  - turn left
  - turn right
  - threat display
  - rest/hover

## Negative guidance

Avoid:

- muddy naturalistic browns unless secondary only
- ragged damaged fins
- hyper-photoreal skin pores or noisy realism
- exaggerated fantasy dragon-fish anatomy
- flat lifeless fins with no elegance
