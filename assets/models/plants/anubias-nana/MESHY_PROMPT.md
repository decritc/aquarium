# Meshy Prompt - Anubias Nana

## Asset ID

`anubias-nana`

## Goal

Create a premium browser-game 3D asset of an Anubias nana plant for a side-view
virtual aquarium game with a **cartoon realism** style.

This plant should feel:

- lush
- sturdy
- broad-leafed
- beginner-friendly
- readable from side view

## Style direction

Follow `/workspace/assets/models/MESHY_STYLE_GUIDE.md`.

The result should look like:

- stylized realism
- bright but believable greens
- clean premium shading
- softly rounded forms
- readable silhouette from gameplay distance

Avoid:

- hyper-detailed botanical realism
- noisy vein detail
- paper-thin flat leaves
- muddy or desaturated color

## Visual description

Create a small freshwater aquarium plant cluster inspired by Anubias nana.

Requirements:

- thick, rounded broad leaves
- compact footprint
- leaves should fan upward and outward
- a visible rhizome/base structure that suggests attachment to wood or rock
- strong side silhouette with 5 to 9 readable leaves
- leaves should feel waxy and healthy without looking plastic

## Rendering and gameplay role

The plant is used in:

- betta display tanks
- planted showcase tanks
- community tanks

It must:

- read well in side-view tanks
- provide visual cover
- work near driftwood or rock
- avoid excessive fine geometry

## Technical guidance

- optimize for browser delivery
- low to moderate poly count
- keep geometry clean and efficient
- use a small material count
- avoid extreme transparency
- origin near the plant base
- model should stand upright naturally

## Prompt for Meshy

Create a stylized-realistic 3D aquarium plant inspired by Anubias nana for a
premium browser aquarium game. The plant should have a compact rhizome base and
5 to 9 thick rounded broad leaves that fan upward and outward. The leaves should
be healthy, lush, bright green, and slightly waxy, with simple believable shading
and minimal fine vein detail. The silhouette must read clearly from a side-view
camera. The style should be cartoon realism: polished, colorful, softly rounded,
and premium rather than hyper-real or flat. Keep the geometry efficient for web
delivery, avoid excessive transparency, and make the asset suitable for placement
near driftwood or rocks in a planted or betta showcase aquarium.

## Output expectations

Produce:

- one main 3D model suitable for export as GLB
- one clean preview render
- one consistent style match with other starter aquarium assets
