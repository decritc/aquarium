# Meshy Prompt: Floating Water Sprite

## Asset goal

Create a stylized-realistic floating water sprite plant for a browser-based virtual
aquarium game. The look should follow a **cartoon realism** direction: believable
plant structure and aquatic readability, but with slightly simplified forms, clean
silhouettes, attractive shading, and premium game-ready color.

This plant is meant to:

- float near the surface
- provide soft cover for betta and guppy setups
- read clearly from a side-view aquarium camera
- look premium and cohesive beside the other starter freshwater assets

## Visual style

- stylized realism / cartoon realism
- bright but natural freshwater greens
- soft, premium shading
- believable plant form without noisy micro-detail
- clean silhouette readable at gameplay distance
- slightly idealized shape and fullness so it looks attractive in a polished game tank

Avoid:

- muddy or desaturated greens
- hyper-photoreal tiny leaf clutter
- low-detail mobile-game flatness
- fantasy glowing plants
- sharp spiky aggressive shapes

## Model description

Model a floating freshwater stem-and-leaf cluster inspired by water sprite.

Key characteristics:

- dense floating top mass
- fine but readable leaf shapes
- slightly drooping underwater leaf strands
- soft organic radial spread at the surface
- attractive fullness without becoming a messy blob

The plant should feel:

- light
- fresh
- healthy
- calm

## Composition and shape requirements

- clear floating top silhouette
- leaf spread should feel broad enough to cast visual cover near the surface
- underwater trailing growth should read from the side view without becoming visually noisy
- not too tall; this is a surface-cover piece, not a background stem forest
- should look good when placed alone or in a small cluster

## Rendering/gameplay context

This plant will be used in:

- betta display tanks
- guppy-friendly setups
- planted or lightly overgrown freshwater tanks

It will be viewed from:

- side-view gameplay camera
- edit-mode orbit and top views

Because of this, the model should:

- look attractive from the top
- show depth and dangling structure from the side
- maintain a clean readable shape at mid-distance

## Material and shading requirements

- one coherent stylized-realistic material set
- premium soft shading with nice light response
- gentle color variation across leaves
- no overly translucent shader dependence
- no unnecessary material fragmentation

## Browser-performance requirements

- optimized for web delivery
- modest geometry count
- no excessive thin alpha cards if avoidable
- prefer readable geometry and strong silhouette over tiny detail

## Output expectations

Produce:

- one centered floating water sprite model suitable for export to GLB
- orientation clean and predictable
- scale appropriate for a small-to-medium aquarium item

Do not include:

- tank
- substrate
- fish
- bubbles
- scene dressing
- multiple separate plant species in the same asset

## Short prompt version

"Create a premium stylized-realistic floating water sprite aquarium plant for a
browser-based virtual aquarium game. The style is cartoon realism: believable
freshwater plant structure with bright healthy greens, clean silhouettes, soft
premium shading, and simplified forms that read clearly in a side-view tank.
Model a surface-floating plant mass with attractive broad leaf coverage and some
underwater trailing leaf structure. It should feel calm, fresh, and beautiful,
supporting betta and guppy tanks. Avoid muddy colors, hyper-photoreal noise, or
flat low-detail shapes. Optimize for browser performance and export as a clean
game-ready single asset."
