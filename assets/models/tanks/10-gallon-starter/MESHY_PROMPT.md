## Prompt for Meshy
Create a stylized-realistic 10-gallon rectangular home aquarium tank shell for a
premium browser aquarium game. The look should be clean, cozy, and believable,
with cartoon realism rather than photorealism. Use clear glass, soft rounded edge
seams, subtle black or dark charcoal trim, and a readable silhouette from a side
view. The tank should feel like a real starter aquarium sold for tropical
community fish or guppy breeder setups.

from free orbit in editor mode, so the shape must read well from front, side, and
angled views. Include simple believable construction details like top rim, bottom
frame, and subtle inner glass thickness, but keep the model efficient and not over-
detailed. Do not include gravel, water, plants, decorations, heater, lids, fish,
labels, brand names, or bubbles. This should be an empty runtime tank shell only.

The proportions should support a wider, more practical aquarium than the 5-gallon
betta display tank, suitable for neon tetra schools, panda corydoras, or a guppy
starter breeder setup. The tank should feel horizontally balanced, readable, and
good for layered side-view fish movement.
