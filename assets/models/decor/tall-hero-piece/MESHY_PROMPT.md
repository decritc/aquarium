# Meshy Prompt: Tall Hero Decor Piece

## Asset role

This is a tall statement decor asset used to validate multi-angle editing and give
the featured observer tank a premium focal point.

Suggested form:

- ruined stone arch
- temple fragment
- stylized stacked stone tower

Choose one coherent direction, but keep it aquarium-safe and premium rather than
cheesy.

## Consistent style requirements

Match the shared style guide at `assets/models/MESHY_STYLE_GUIDE.md`.

Target style:

- cartoon realism
- believable proportions
- strong side-view silhouette
- warm premium shading
- bright readable surfaces without realism-grime overload

## Primary prompt

Create a premium aquarium decor model for a browser-based virtual aquarium game.
The asset should be a tall statement piece that reads clearly in side view and
looks impressive in a cozy planted or showcase tank. Preferred direction: a
stylized ruined stone arch or temple fragment with smooth softened edges, subtle
erosion, aquarium-safe shapes, and a premium cartoon-realism finish. The object
should feel handcrafted and elegant, not spooky, muddy, or hyper-real. Use clean
stone forms with layered depth, readable openings, and strong silhouette. It must
look good from top, side, and free-orbit inspection because the tank editor allows
multi-angle placement review. Materials should be optimized for browser use and not
depend on excessive tiny details.

## Geometry and render guidance

- Strong silhouette from side view
- Readable negative space if using an arch form
- Enough depth to matter in orbit view
- Avoid thin fragile spires that read poorly
- Keep mesh game-ready and reasonably optimized

## Placement and tank usage

- Heavy bottom-set decor
- Used as a centerpiece or focal point
- Should feel plausible in a freshwater showcase tank
- Must not visually dominate so much that fish disappear around it

## Output expectations

- One clean GLB model
- Centered/clean transform
- Good pivot/origin for bottom placement
- One preview render

## Negative prompt

Do not generate:

- realistic broken ruins with heavy grime
- horror or dungeon vibes
- massive scale out of proportion to a small/medium aquarium
- hyper-detailed sculpt noise that disappears in browser play
- overly toy-like plastic styling
