# Meshy Prompt: River Rock Cluster

## Asset ID

- `river-rock-cluster`

## Asset purpose

Starter hardscape for freshwater community and planted tanks.

## Prompt

Create a stylized-realistic cluster of smooth freshwater river stones for a browser
aquarium game. The stones should feel believable and naturally rounded, but the
overall silhouette should be clean, readable, and slightly art-directed for a
premium cozy game. Aim for "cartoon realism": simplified forms, polished shading,
soft edge highlights, attractive material breakup, and no noisy micro-detail.

The cluster should read well from a fixed side-view camera. Build a composition of
several stones of varying sizes stacked into a stable low-to-mid-height hardscape
piece that can sit on aquarium substrate. The front silhouette should be visually
interesting, with a few height steps, but it should not become a huge wall that
blocks the entire lower tank. The side-view shape should give fish and plants room
to be seen around it. The top-down footprint should feel natural and organic rather
than perfectly circular.

Color palette should use attractive natural stone grays with subtle warm and cool
variation: soft slate gray, muted taupe gray, river-washed blue-gray undertones.
The rocks should feel clean and premium, not dirty or photorealistically rough.
Keep specular response restrained and believable for wet rock in aquarium lighting,
with smooth polished shading and readable form.

The model should have:
- clean readable silhouette
- no sharp dangerous points
- low-to-mid vertical profile
- stable bottom contact
- enough depth variation to support fish swimming in front of or behind nearby decor
- browser-friendly geometry and materials

Do not include substrate, plants, fish, or tank glass. Deliver only the rock
cluster asset.

## Look and feel keywords

- cartoon realism
- premium browser game asset
- soft polished shading
- readable silhouette
- natural freshwater hardscape
- cozy planted tank

## Modeling constraints

- one combined placeable prop
- centered and grounded for easy placement
- stable bottom footprint
- optimized for web rendering
- avoid excessive material complexity

## Animation

No animation needed.

## Output expectation

Produce:
- one runtime-ready rock cluster model
- one clean textured asset with coherent materials
- one side-view-friendly silhouette suitable for starter tank layouts
